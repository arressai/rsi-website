FROM ASH I BLOOM is an immersive sonic experience that explores the concept of emergence through stillness and activation of the power of femininity. Focus is illuminated through the light sculpture at the center of the room where argon light outlines a form reminiscent of a woman's hips. The work comments on the birth / rebirth life cycle, and offers a meditative initiation into a series of immersive sonic activations for attendees.

Drawing on visuals from an other-world, the artist uses activations as a method of transportation for healing story telling, and experiential revelation.
